# Round 2 — cross-critique of the proposed process

Author-informed review of `proposed-process.txt`, `round1-educator.md`, and `round1-process.md`. I authored repair components. No independent learner evidence, course edits, or skill edits.

## Accept, with concrete amendments

**Accept one canonical protocol and distinct evidence claims.** The process review establishes that relevant rules already existed. Consolidation can reduce contradictory execution; adding more rules alone cannot establish compliance. The recorder should distinguish “required action recorded,” “artifact reviewed,” “interaction observed,” and “learning measured.” An honest unverified learning claim must not prevent delivering a clearly labeled prototype.

**Accept an outer strategy loop and an inner inference loop.** The educator report's strongest observation is the delayed connection between the motivating error criterion and the variance objective. The outer loop must test whether the route answers the learner's original question. The inner loop must test the explanation of a transition. Passing many local nodes cannot establish a coherent whole. Add one cold walkthrough that asks what problem the course solves and why each major step belongs.

**Accept worked examples before unsupported discovery for novices.** “Predict” need not mean “invent the missing mathematics.” Permit a low-cost qualitative prediction, then explicit instruction and supported practice. For unfamiliar content, “I do not know” is useful diagnostic information. Do not turn every reveal into a compulsory assessment or every correct answer into an interrogation.

**Accept bounded repair attempts, not a universal two-attempt law.** Count attempts against a stable failure and intended observation. Two CSS retries and two failed explanatory models differ. A cap should stop reproducing the same failure, preserve the draft, and trigger a scope or representation decision. It cannot force success or silently authorize more work.

## Reject or narrow these proposals

**Reject causal/generative models as the only permissible form.** Some topics primarily concern classifications, conventions, ethical arguments, interpretation, or procedural fluency. Require an appropriate usable structure: causal mechanism, explanatory argument, comparison, decision rule, or procedure. “Purpose first” also need not require an invented commercial use; a clear intellectual puzzle can provide sufficient purpose.

**Narrow “freeze route only after evidence.”** Specify the evidence and its authority. Source inspection and a fresh-context AI walkthrough can justify proceeding with a pilot; they cannot establish human learning. Require strongest-objection and falsification records for consequential choices, not a form for every sentence. Three graphs are internal implementation options, not three mandatory authoring deliverables.

**Reject treating retrieval as a substitute for intuition or as an efficiency guarantee.** The verified 2006 finding concerns delayed recall of prose after study/testing schedules, with different period durations. It does not show that repeated retrieval creates a missing mental model or proves proof comprehension. Use retrieval to recover and apply a model that was taught; use new counterfactuals and justifications to examine whether the model works. Delayed retrieval and delayed transfer require separate tasks and claims.

**Narrow independent/creator-blind review language.** Fresh context reduces exposure to the author's diagnosis; it does not erase shared model training, existing topic knowledge, or correlated mistakes. Reviewer identity and prior involvement must remain visible. Brand removal may reduce expectation effects, but recognizable voices and visual styles may prevent creator blinding. Record actual blinding rather than intent.

## A fair comparator and what it costs

Match a bounded learning obligation, preparation, assessment, and available time. Include navigation and interaction overhead in time; allow video pause/replay. Do not compare our whole course with a video's opening, or silently omit our difficult proof from the assessment. If evaluating the broad route, use a corresponding sequence of reference material and report the resulting scope.

Same-topic sequential exposure contaminates a learning comparison; counterbalancing does not remove that carryover. For actual learners, consider separate randomized groups or matched alternative tasks. Blind scoring where feasible and separate preference, immediate task performance, delayed retention, and transfer. A few expert inspections can find defects cheaply; increasingly elaborate review paperwork has diminishing value. Repeat reviews only after a relevant change or unresolved disagreement. Human studies cost recruitment, time, and scoring; a skill must not imply they happened automatically.

## Round 3 benchmark claims

Unseen quantitative and nonmathematical pilot tasks can test whether the revised skill produces concrete purpose, coherent representations, earned formal steps, scoped research, conditional feedback, and honest stopping behavior outside PCA. Retrospective-only and brief-answer cases test scope discipline. An inaccessible-video case tests truthful provenance. Injected failures test whether the recorder blocks stale or unresolved evidence.

These pilots cannot establish human intuition, durable retention, universal topic coverage, or superiority to expert YouTube teaching. A knowledgeable AI reviewer may solve a task from prior knowledge despite a bad lesson. Require it to identify the learner-visible warrant for its answer and label that result an explanation audit, not a novice learning gain. Report this benchmark as evidence of process behavior and specific artifact properties, with human efficacy still unverified.
