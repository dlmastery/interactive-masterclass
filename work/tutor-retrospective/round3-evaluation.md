## Supported improvement and limits

No new teaching rule or course repair is justified by these outputs. The smallest useful process addition, if not already present, is one required label on each forward-test result: **written response inspected**, **execution independently verified**, or **learner performance observed**. The retrospective scope case illustrates why these cannot share a single pass label. Record the exact skill revision and candidate context alongside the existing frozen hashes.

These results support applicability of the proposed approach to two bounded explanations and four narrow behaviors. They do not establish novices’ understanding, delayed retention, comparative superiority, robustness on a full course, or repair of the PCA course. No blocking content flaw was found within this test scope.
