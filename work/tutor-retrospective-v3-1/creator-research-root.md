## StatQuest: https://statquest.org/about

Inspected the About StatQuest section. Josh Starmer describes explaining statistics to genetics researchers using pictures and removing terminology that obscured the ideas. Proposed adaptation: establish the relationship in a visible case before naming it, then explicitly reconnect the picture to the technical vocabulary and equations needed for the chosen depth. This is a creator's account, not a controlled efficacy result. The source does not justify omitting the mathematics the user requested. The trailing-slash URL failed; the canonical URL above loaded.

Proposed adaptation: track why each conceptual step is needed and return to the originating question; end with opportunities to formulate and inspect a new question. ## Nicky Case: https://blog.ncase.me/explorable-explanations-4-more-design-patterns/

Inspected puzzle, prediction, role-play, and sandbox sections. The source distinguishes operating controls from doing explanatory work and notes overload risks in open exploration. Proposed adaptation: ask the learner to make a prediction or construct a case, then let the observable result inform a reasoned revision. Open exploration should follow usable entry knowledge or provide a modest initial task. A simulator's output is not empirical evidence about the real world, and agreement with its coded model is not proof of the model.
