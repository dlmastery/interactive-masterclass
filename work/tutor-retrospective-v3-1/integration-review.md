# Focused v3.1 integration review

Author-informed review of the installed references and their canonical/sibling links; not a blind forward test. I inspected explanation-depth, explainer-craft, lesson-design, review, narration-production, adaptive-tutoring, both sibling entrypoints, and the existing Welch access manifest. No skill or course edits were made.

1. **Separate the learning person's readiness from the explanation recipient's readiness.** `explanation-depth.md:28` records “learner prerequisites,” while its teach-back tasks require explaining to a specified audience. These are different contracts: an undergraduate may be learning to explain to a child, while an expert outside the field may need an elementary account themselves. `adaptive-tutoring.md:7–11` does not explicitly preserve this distinction. Add the explanation recipient and assumed knowledge to the existing task state, separately from the student's demonstrated preparation; link the depth reference from the tutoring reference. Otherwise a technically correct answer using calculus could pass an age-10 teach-back, or a gap in the student's own knowledge could be mistaken for poor audience adaptation. Recheck with one task in which the two readiness levels differ; do not create six new state machines.

2. **Make prompt/response fields conditional at individual cues.** `narration-production.md:17–26` says every meaningful scene/cue includes a learner prompt and response. `explainer-craft.md` correctly rejects compulsory interaction and permits stable representations or direct explanation. Qualify the prompt field as applicable when the scene has a learner task; a cue may simply develop the argument or give inspection time. Preserve exact spoken notes, visual state, and useful pacing. This resolves the remaining overprescription without weakening the user's narration requirement. The new allowance for natural phrasing is otherwise compatible with reviewed exact notes.

3. **Make the Welch source key reproduce the inspection already performed.** `explainer-craft.md:65` links the repository but tells future readers to inspect specific notebooks without naming those inspected for these claims. Add Parts 1, 3, 5, and 7 and “markdown/selected code read, not executed,” matching the existing access manifest; retain the source snapshot in the review package. No new research artifact is needed. This helps distinguish an observed sequence from a proposed adaptation or merely recognizing the channel name.

Keep the present safeguards: shared core and explicit simplification boundaries; independent learner attempts rather than six author scripts; domain-specific rigor; scope-limited construction; and text/static-image inspection distinct from heard performance. No unsupported claim of achieved learning or creator endorsement was located. Integration review establishes written consistency only; the six-lens behavior remains unverified until sampled with learner-facing output and audience-specific follow-ups.
