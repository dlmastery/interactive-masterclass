# Build an intuition-first masterclass

Use the single maintained protocol in [build-interactive-masterclass](../build-interactive-masterclass/SKILL.md). Read it before substantial tutorial work. Do not execute two research/review pipelines when both skills are selected.

Start with why the learner needs the idea and what it enables. Introduce background when it unlocks a needed inference. Preserve the requested depth and test transfer before revealing its answer.

Apply version 3's complete coverage: why/when/where, mechanism and justification, applications and current uses, limitations with diagnostics and mitigations, alternatives, best practices, and transfer. For flagship work, the user's named explainers seed at least 30 relevant video candidates where available; access depth stays explicit. Quality takes priority over arbitrary duration or slide count. Read the canonical narration/production reference for speaker notes and public video work.

Honor scope: an assessment or process update does not authorize rebuilding the lesson. Small answers and edits do not need the full pipeline.

For student questions, branching feedback, or per-problem state, also read [adaptive tutoring](../build-interactive-masterclass/references/adaptive-tutoring.md). Old links in [teaching-contract.md](references/teaching-contract.md) route to these maintained references.

If the sibling skill is missing, recover it from the same installed bundle or report the missing dependency. Do not silently substitute an obsolete process.
